# I forgot my password or can't log in

**What this article helps with:** Getting back into your BrightPath account if you can't remember your password or your login isn't working.

## Why this happens

This is very common and easy to fix. It usually happens because the password was typed incorrectly, Caps Lock is on, or the account hasn't been used for a while. Your account and information are safe.

## How to reset your password

1. Go to the BrightPath login page.
2. Click **"Forgot password?"** below the login button.
3. Enter the email address or phone number linked to your account.
4. Click **"Send reset link"**.
5. Check your email inbox (or your phone for an SMS). If you don't see it within 5 minutes, check your **Spam or Junk** folder.
6. Open the message and click the reset link. The link works for 30 minutes only.
7. Create a new password with at least 8 characters, including a letter, a number, and a symbol.
8. Return to the login page and sign in with your new password.

**Before you reset, quickly check these:**

- Caps Lock is off.
- You're using the email or phone number you registered with.
- Students: log in with your student ID if your school uses one.
- Your internet connection is working.

## If it still doesn't work

- **Didn't get the reset message:** make sure the email or phone number is spelled correctly, wait 10 minutes, then try once more. Avoid clicking the button many times, as this can delay the message.
- **Link says "expired":** go back to Step 2 and request a new link.
- **Account locked:** after several wrong attempts, accounts lock for 30 minutes for your safety. Wait, then try again.

If none of this works, contact us and include:

- Your full name
- Your student ID (students) or your child's name and class (parents)
- The email or phone number you used to register
- A screenshot of any error message

## How to contact support

- **Email:** support@brightpath.example
- **In-portal chat:** click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

For your safety, we will never ask for your password. Don't share it with anyone, including staff.
