# I think someone else has accessed my account

**What this article helps with:** What to do if you notice unusual activity on your BrightPath account, or you think someone else knows your password.

## Signs your account may be at risk

- You received a password reset email or verification code that you did not request.
- You see a login at a time or place you don't recognise.
- Your phone number, email or password was changed and you did not do it.
- Messages, payments or submissions appear that you did not make.
- You clicked a link or entered your details on a page that looked like BrightPath but felt suspicious.

If any of this applies, act quickly. You can usually fix it in a few minutes.

## What to do right now

1. **Change your password.** Go to **Profile**, then **Security**, then **Change password**. Choose a new password that you have not used anywhere else. Use at least 8 characters, with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols.
2. **Log out of all devices.** In **Security**, click **Sign out of all devices**.
3. **Check your contact details.** Make sure the phone number and email on your account are yours. Correct any you don't recognise.
4. **Review recent activity.** If your account shows a login history, look for logins you don't recognise and write down the date and time.
5. **Turn on two-step verification** if it is available on your account.
6. **Change the password on any other account where you used the same password,** especially your email.
7. **Contact support** with the details below, so we can review the account.

## If you can't log in

If your password has been changed, use **Forgot password?** on the login page. If the reset message goes to a phone number or email you don't control, contact support immediately.

## What to send support

- Your full name, class or your child's name and class
- What you noticed, and when
- Dates and times of any logins you don't recognise
- Screenshots of suspicious messages or emails (please do not include your password)

## What we will do

- Lock the account to stop further access
- Review recent login and activity records
- Confirm your identity before restoring access
- Pass serious cases to the school's IT and security team

## How to contact support

- **Email:** support@brightpath.example
- **In-portal chat:** click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

## Staying safe

- Never share your password or verification code with anyone. BrightPath staff will never ask for either.
- Only log in through the official BrightPath website or app. Check the web address carefully before entering your details.
- Be careful with links in messages that ask you to "verify your account" or pay urgently.
- Log out when using a shared or public computer.
