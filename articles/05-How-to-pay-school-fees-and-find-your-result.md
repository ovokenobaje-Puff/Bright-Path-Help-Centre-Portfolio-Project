# How to pay school fees and find your receipt

**What this article helps with:** Paying school fees through the BrightPath portal and finding or downloading your payment receipt afterwards.

## Before you start

You need a parent account linked to your child, your child's student ID, and either a bank app for transfers or a debit card for card payments.

## How to pay school fees

1. Log in to the BrightPath portal.
2. Click **Fees** or **Payments** in the main menu.
3. If you have more than one child, select the right child from the list at the top.
4. Check the fee breakdown and the total amount due.
5. Click **Pay now** and choose your payment method:
   - **Bank transfer:** the portal shows an account number and a payment reference. Send the exact amount from your bank app and use the reference given.
   - **Card:** enter your card details on the secure payment page and confirm.
6. Wait for the confirmation message. For bank transfers, confirmation can take a few minutes.
7. Keep a screenshot or photo of the confirmation for your own records.

## How to find your receipt

1. Click **Payments**, then **Transaction History**.
2. Find the payment by date or amount.
3. Click **View receipt** or **Download**.
4. Save the receipt as a PDF or print it, and keep it until the school confirms your records are updated.

## If it still doesn't work

- **No "Pay now" button:** the fee may already be paid or not yet published. Check **Transaction History**, or ask the school office.
- **Card payment fails:** check your card balance and that online payments are enabled, then try again. Do not retry many times in a row, as this can cause duplicate charges.
- **Receipt not showing:** payments can take time to confirm. Wait 30 minutes and refresh. If it still doesn't appear, see our article on paid fees showing as unpaid.

For your safety, only pay through the BrightPath portal. We will never ask you to send money to a personal account or share your card PIN or OTP.
