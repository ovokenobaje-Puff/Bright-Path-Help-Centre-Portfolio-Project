# I can't see my results or report card

**What this article helps with:** Finding your exam results or report card in the BrightPath portal when they don't appear on your account.

## Why this happens

Your results are safe. The most common reasons they don't show are:

- Your teachers haven't finished uploading and approving them yet.
- The wrong term or session is selected on the results page.
- Your school has not released results for your class yet.
- The page needs refreshing after a recent update.

## How to find your results

1. Log in to the BrightPath portal.
2. Click **Results** (or **Report Card**) in the main menu.
3. Check the **Session** and **Term** boxes at the top of the page. Select the term you are looking for, for example "First Term 2026/2027".
4. Click **View** or **Search**.
5. If the page is blank, refresh it, then close and reopen your browser.
6. Try a different browser (for example Chrome) or the BrightPath mobile app.

**Quick checks:**

- You are logged in with your own student account.
- Your internet connection is stable.
- Your school has announced that results are ready.

## If it still doesn't work

- **Page says "No results available":** your teachers may still be uploading scores. Check your school's announcements, or ask your class teacher whether results for your class have been released.
- **Some subjects are missing:** a teacher may not have uploaded that subject yet. Tell your class teacher which subjects are missing.
- **Page won't load or shows an error:** clear your browser's saved data (cache), then try again.

If none of this works, contact us and include:

- Your full name and class
- Your student ID
- The term and session you are trying to view
- A screenshot of what you see on the page

## How to contact support

- **Email:** support@brightpath.example
- **In-portal chat:** click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

Please note that support cannot change or add scores. Only your teachers and school office can do that.
