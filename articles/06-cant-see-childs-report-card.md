# I can't see my child's report card or my child's profile

**What this article helps with:** Finding your child's report card in the BrightPath portal when it doesn't appear, or when your child's name is missing from your account.

## Why this happens

Your child's records are safe. The most common reasons are that the wrong child or term is selected, results have not been released yet, or your child has not been linked to your parent account.

## How to find your child's report card

1. Log in to your parent account.
2. At the top of the page, check which child is selected. If you have more than one, use the **Switch child** menu.
3. Click **Report Card** or **Results**.
4. Select the correct **Session** and **Term**.
5. Click **View**. If the page is blank, refresh it and try again.

**Quick checks:**

- You are logged in to your own parent account, not your child's student account.
- The school has announced that report cards are ready.
- Your internet connection is stable.

## If it still doesn't work

- **Message says "Results not available":** the school may not have released them yet. Check school announcements or ask the class teacher.
- **Report card is locked:** some schools release report cards only after certain requirements are met, such as cleared fees. Check the **Fees** page or ask the school office.
- **Your child's name is missing from your account:** your child may not be linked yet. See our article on linking a child to your account.
- **The wrong child's information shows:** log out, log in again, and use **Switch child**. If it still looks wrong, contact us right away.

When you contact us, please include:

- Your full name and the email or phone number on your account
- Your child's full name, class and student ID
- The term and session you are trying to view
- A screenshot of what you see

## How to contact support

- **Email:** support@brightpath.example
- **In-portal chat:** click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

Please note that support cannot change grades or release report cards. Only the school can do that.
