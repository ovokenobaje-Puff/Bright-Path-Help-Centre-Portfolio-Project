# I didn't receive my verification code or reset email

**What this article helps with:** Getting your BrightPath verification code (OTP) by SMS, or your password reset email, when it doesn't arrive.

## Why this happens

Your account is fine. Messages can be delayed by network problems, filtered into spam, or sent to an old phone number or email address.

## What to try

1. **Wait 2 minutes.** Codes usually arrive in under a minute, but networks can be slow at busy times.
2. **Check your details.** Make sure the phone number or email on your account is correct, with no missing digit or typo.
3. **For SMS codes:**
   - Check that your phone has network signal and enough storage space.
   - Turn off Do Not Disturb, and check any blocked-messages or spam folder in your messaging app.
   - Restart your phone, then request a new code.
4. **For email:**
   - Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder, and the **Promotions** tab if you use Gmail.
   - Search your inbox for "BrightPath".
5. Wait at least 60 seconds, then click **Resend code**. Only use the newest code, because older ones stop working.

**Please avoid:**

- Pressing "Resend" many times in a row. This can delay messages and temporarily block new requests.
- Sharing your code with anyone, including school staff. We will never ask for it.

## If it still doesn't work

- **Still nothing after 10 minutes:** your phone number or email on file may be outdated. If you can log in another way, update it under **Profile**. See our article on updating your phone number or email.
- **You no longer have access to that number or email:** contact support so we can verify your identity and help.
- **Your account is temporarily locked:** too many requests can trigger a short lock. Wait 30 minutes, then try once more.

When you contact us, please include:

- Your full name, class or child's name and class
- The phone number or email you are using (hide part of it if you prefer)
- The time you last requested a code
- Your mobile network, such as MTN, Airtel, Glo or 9mobile
