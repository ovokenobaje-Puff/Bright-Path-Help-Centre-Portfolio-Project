# How to link a second child to my account

**What this article helps with:** Adding another child to your BrightPath parent account so you can view their results and pay their fees from one login.

## Before you start

You need:

- Your child's student ID
- The **link code** from the school. The school office gives this to parents when a child is registered.
- Access to the phone number or email on your parent account, in case we send a verification code.

## How to link a child

1. Log in to your parent account.
2. Click **My Children** in the main menu.
3. Click **Add child**.
4. Enter your child's **student ID** and the **link code** from the school.
5. Click **Link child**.
6. If the portal sends a verification code, enter it to confirm that it is really you.
7. Your child's name should now appear at the top of the page. Use **Switch child** to move between your children.

## If it still doesn't work

- **"Invalid code" message:** check the student ID and code for typing mistakes. Codes may expire, so ask the school office for a new one.
- **"Child already linked" message:** another parent or guardian may have linked this child. Contact the school office to confirm who the registered contact is.
- **You don't have a link code:** visit or call the school office. For privacy and safety, support cannot link a child without confirmation from the school.
- **You can't find "My Children":** make sure you are logged in with a parent account, not a student account.

When you contact us, please include:

- Your full name and the email or phone number on your parent account
- Your child's full name and class
- A screenshot of any error message (please do not include the link code)

## How to contact support

- **Email:** support@brightpath.example
- **In-portal chat:** click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

To protect every student's information, we only link a child to a parent after the school has confirmed the relationship.
