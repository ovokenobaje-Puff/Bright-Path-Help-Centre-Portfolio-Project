# How to update my phone number or email address

**What this article helps with:** Changing the phone number or email address linked to your BrightPath account, so you keep getting codes, reset links and school notices.

## Why this is important

BrightPath sends verification codes and password reset links to your phone number or email. If these details are old, you could be locked out, or someone else could receive messages about your account.

## How to update your details

1. Log in to your account.
2. Click your name or profile picture, then **Profile** or **Account Settings**.
3. Find **Phone number** or **Email address** and click **Edit**.
4. Enter the new phone number or email address. Include the full phone number, for example 0803 xxx xxxx.
5. Click **Save**.
6. A verification code will be sent to your **new** number or email. Enter it to confirm the change.
7. You may also be asked to enter your password, as an extra security step.
8. Log out and log in again to check that the new details are saved.

## If it still doesn't work

- **You don't receive the code on your new number or email:** check for typing mistakes, then see our article on verification codes that don't arrive.
- **You can't log in at all:** use **Forgot password?** on the login page. See our article on forgetting your password.
- **You no longer have access to your old number or email:** contact support. We will need to confirm your identity before changing anything.
- **Students:** if you cannot change your own details, ask a parent or the school office, as some accounts are managed by the school.

To confirm your identity, we may ask for:

- Your full name, class and student ID (or your child's details if you are a parent)
- Information only the account holder should know, such as the school's name and registration details
- Confirmation from the school office

## How to contact support

- **Email:** support@brightpath.example
- **In-portal chat:** click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

For your safety, we will never change your contact details without checking your identity, and we will never ask for your password or verification code.
