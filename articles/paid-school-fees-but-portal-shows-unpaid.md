I paid school fees but the portal still shows "unpaid"

What this article helps with: What to do when you have made a school fee payment but the BrightPath portal still shows the fee as unpaid or pending.

Why this happens

Your money is safe. Payments often take a short time to be confirmed, especially bank transfers made at busy times or outside banking hours. A payment can also fail to match a student record if the payment reference was entered incorrectly.

What to do
Wait at least 30 minutes after paying. Bank transfers can take longer, sometimes up to 24 hours.
Log in to the portal and click Payments, then Transaction History.
Look for your payment. A status of Pending means it is still being confirmed.
Log out, log in again, and refresh the page.
Check your bank app or receipt and confirm the money has left your account. Keep the receipt or screenshot.
If the portal still shows "unpaid" after 24 hours, contact support with the details below.

Quick checks:

You paid to the account number shown in the portal, not an old or saved one.
You used the student's ID or the payment reference shown in the portal.
The amount you paid matches the amount due.
If it still doesn't work
Money left your account but no record in the portal: send us your proof of payment so we can trace it.
You paid the wrong amount: contact the school bursar's office. Support cannot adjust fee amounts.
You paid twice: send us both receipts. Duplicate payments are reviewed and refunded or credited by the school.

When you contact us, please include:

Your full name and your child's full name, class and student ID
The date, time and amount of the payment
The payment reference or transaction number
A screenshot or photo of the bank receipt (please hide your full account number)
How to contact support
Email: support@brightpath.example
In-portal chat: click the chat icon at the bottom right of the portal
Hours: Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm

Please note that support cannot mark a fee as paid without verification. The school bursar confirms all payments.
